2\. What is Consulting?

30:25

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WgwnDW23guQ)

Consulting is not a dude who went to an Ivy League school and wears a blue suit smiling like they belong in a toothpaste commercial while reading a 200 page slide deck.  
  
In my neck of the woods, a consultant is someone who is an expert in their field and gets paid a maximum rate for the work that they deliver to a client. That work could be technical or advisory, or both. In my experience, it's been both, and it's your job to bridge that gap so that your client gets exactly the outcome that they need.
