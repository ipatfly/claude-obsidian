# E-commerce SEO

E-commerce SEO refers to the optimization of online retail stores and product catalogs to improve search engine rankings, drive organic traffic, and optimize conversion pathways. Within the context of Claude SEO, it represents a specialized analytical domain for auditing product collections, category templates, and user intent.
